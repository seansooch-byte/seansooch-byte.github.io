The Lab: molecule primitives (lab/kit/art/molecules.js)

Ions, small molecules, transmitters, peptides, steroids and drug glyphs at 480 px, at the phone width (375 px) and small (200 px), with their parameters swept. Not linked; tools/ is never shipped. ?only=<section id>[,id] shows chosen sections; ?w=480,375 chooses widths.
