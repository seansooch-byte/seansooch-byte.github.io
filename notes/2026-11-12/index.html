SpaceX talks about absorbing Tesla

Bloomberg reports that SpaceX and Tesla are negotiating a combination that could be announced in December, with the talks now on price and control. Electrek, citing that report, says both boards formed special committees in October, that the structure under discussion has SpaceX absorbing Tesla, and that one open question is how to separate Tesla's China business. Tesla shares rose as much as 1.9%. Neither company has commented. The report rests on unnamed sources.

SpaceX bought xAI earlier this year, and xAI makes Grok. This site is built with Claude and Grok. Coverage says that if it happens, it would be the largest corporate deal on record, joining a rocket maker, a car maker and an AI lab under one owner. Tesla's annual shareholder meeting is on December 3. Nobody has confirmed the talks.

Anthropic is rolling Claude Meetings out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, for now only on Google Meet. Six models and a chip the rules bar

Will DePue, a former OpenAI researcher posting as Unemployment Labs, says six open-weight Chinese models started on Nvidia Blackwell chips that US export rules bar from China. He says he searched more than 6 trillion combinations and estimates 2,000 to 3,000 chips for the largest runs. He says OpenAI and Anthropic researchers verified it, with no outside reviewer named. Nvidia, DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group had not commented. He says the method does not show a law was broken.
