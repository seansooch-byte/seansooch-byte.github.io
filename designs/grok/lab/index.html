The Lab

A course in core medicine, taught one picture at a time. Each lesson takes a single idea, starting with how the heart fills and empties, builds it step by step in plain language with animated visuals, then shows how disease and common drug classes change the picture. New lessons are added in order.

Ground rules

Every lesson has a Clinical values tab: typical adult reference ranges from standard physiology textbooks and major guidelines, each one cited, drawn against the lesson as it runs. The lessons are educational only and not for clinical use; they give no drug doses. Everything runs in your browser, with no accounts and no trackers, and works on a phone and with a keyboard.
