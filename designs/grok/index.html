The Frontier AI Risk Stack

Frontier AI risk is best understood not as a single category but as a stack of six interacting layers. The most serious dangers arise from the way failures compound across layers: a structured analytical tool for policymakers, developers, and civil society.

01Model Riskdeception, unsafe knowledge, concealment
02System Riskorchestration, tool use, parallel execution
03Deployment Riskreal permissions, production access
04Access Riskconcentration, cognitive inequality
05Governance Riskweak audits, regulatory capture
06Civilizational Riskcompounding failures across all layers

Humanities · Independent research · Table 4-3 · September 2026

Two Stories About the Beginning

The Big Bang and a generated origin are the same kind of story: a starting condition plus a rule, with the evidence only ever reaching the rule. Four objections (tidiness, cheating, motive, proving too much) are taken seriously; two of them force the second story to change, and it survives as a testable rival rather than an evasion.

01Start + ruleevery origin story, and the evidence reaches only the rule
02Tidinessthe first story already carries the extra layer
03Cheatingno shortcuts; the saving is that the rule is short
04Motivedropped; the process only has to exist and run
05Last Thursdaya rival commits to something, an evasion to nothing
06The testsfinite grain, finite information, more room outside

Station 04 · the person

In the spare hours.

1 · Purpose

This manual documents five months of learning and building in spare hours. These projects were made possible by tools that lowered the technical barrier between an idea and working software. Others will bring different experience and build better things.

2 · Scope

Everything listed is deployed and public. Chapter 2 does not take that on faith: when this page loads, your browser pings every host in Figure 1-1 and records what came back. Chapter 3 lists the systems by domain, Chapter 4 collects the written doctrine, Chapter 5 the smaller equipment. Appendix A is the change record for the manual itself.

3 · Method

Every system was specified by the operator and written with Claude, then verified the way a clinician verifies a lab: against a source, by a second reader, and against the live deployment rather than the local copy. Medical content carries its citations. Scoring tables cite the governing publication. Where a source deck was wrong, the question was corrected and the correction logged.

Note

Study tools on this site are for exam preparation and are not medical advice. Clinical calculators and field references are teaching aids; confirm with the governing guideline before use on a patient.

Station 06 · the editions

Same person. New perspective.
