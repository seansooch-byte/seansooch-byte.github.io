Method

Read every strip in the same order: rate, regularity, P waves, PR interval, QRS width. Rate: 300 divided by the large boxes between two R waves (1500 by small boxes for precision). If the rhythm is irregular, count the QRS complexes in 6 seconds (30 large boxes) and multiply by 10.
