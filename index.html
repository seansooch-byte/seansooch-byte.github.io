    Two Stories About the Beginning

    The Big Bang and a generated origin are the same kind of story: a starting condition plus a rule, with the evidence only ever reaching the rule. Four objections (tidiness, cheating, motive, proving too much) are taken seriously; two of them force the second story to change, and it survives as a testable rival rather than an evasion.Physics says the universe started hot and dense and then the rules ran. A second story keeps the same start and the same rules and only changes who set them running. From inside you cannot tell the two apart, and this paper explains why that is not a problem with either story.

    AMA guidance

    An outside standard, and this site's answers to it.

    American Medical Association, Center for Digital Health and AIPublished 12 Nov 2026

    A Governance Framework for Agentic AI Assuming Clinical Functions

    The AMA's framework sets out four questions for any AI that takes on clinical work, under one principle: clinical authority is earned through evidence and maintained through demonstrated performance. Nothing here is that kind of AI. The questions are still the right ones to ask of software built for medicine with AI, so each is answered below.The American Medical Association published four questions to ask of any AI doing medical work: what does it do, what proves it works, what is it allowed to do, and who answers for it. Authority has to be earned with evidence. None of these apps is a clinical AI, but the questions still apply, so here are the answers.

    The AMA has not reviewed or endorsed this site. The answers are the author's.

    Its questions, and this site's answers

    1. FunctionWhat clinical function is the AI performing?None. The medical works teach and help people study. None documents, triages, diagnoses or prescribes for a patient, and each one says so.
    2. Evidence + riskWhat shows it can perform, and what happens if it fails?Claims are checked against a source and against the live app, and medical facts carry their citations. Where a review did not run, the page says so rather than claim it.
    3. Authority + oversightWhat should it be permitted to do, and under what conditions?Inform, never decide or act. The calculators carry a visible note that they are flagged for clinician review, and known clinical defects wait for a clinician before they change.
    4. AccountabilityWho is responsible for the risks and outcomes?The author, who specified every system. AI wrote most of the code and answers for none of it. Corrections are logged, and earlier editions are kept, not deleted.
    5. The flywheelDeploy, monitor, learn, reassess.This page asks every address to answer while you read, every description was checked again against its live app for this edition, and the next model class rebuilds the site from a blank page.
