04 / THE PERSON

In the spare hours.

1-1.Purpose

This manual documents five months of learning and building in spare hours. These projects were made possible by tools that lowered the technical barrier between an idea and working software. Others will bring different experience and build better things.

1-2.Scope

Everything listed is deployed and public. Chapter 2 does not take that on faith: when this page loads, your browser pings every host in Figure 1-1 and records what came back. Chapter 3 lists the systems by domain, Chapter 4 collects the written doctrine, Chapter 5 the smaller equipment. Appendix A is the change record for the manual itself.

1-3.Method

Every system was specified by the operator and written with Claude, then verified the way a clinician verifies a lab: against a source, by a second reader, and against the live deployment rather than the local copy. Medical content carries its citations. Scoring tables cite the governing publication. Where a source deck was wrong, the question was corrected and the correction logged.

NOTE

Study tools on this site are for exam preparation and are not medical advice. Clinical calculators and field references are teaching aids; confirm with the governing guideline before use on a patient.

Same person. New perspective.

one redesign per model class · the site is the benchmark

This site is rebuilt from the ground up at every new Anthropic model class, as a personal benchmark of frontier capability. Each edition is designed end to end by the model it is named for, then retired, not deleted, when its successor arrives.
