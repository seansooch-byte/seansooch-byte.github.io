Two Stories About the Beginning

The Big Bang and a generated origin are the same kind of story: a starting condition plus a rule, with the evidence only ever reaching the rule. Four objections (tidiness, cheating, motive, proving too much) are taken seriously; two of them force the second story to change, and it survives as a testable rival rather than an evasion.Physics says the universe started hot and dense and then the rules ran. A second story keeps the same start and the same rules and only changes who set them running. From inside you cannot tell the two apart, and this paper explains why that is not a problem with either story.
