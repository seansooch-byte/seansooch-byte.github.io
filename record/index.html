Editions

One redesign per model class. Nothing is deleted.

This site is rebuilt from the ground up at every new Anthropic model class, as a personal benchmark of frontier capability. Each edition is designed end to end by the model it is named for, then retired, not deleted, when its successor arrives. The archive is the measurement: put two volumes side by side and you can see, in typography and judgment rather than benchmark scores, how far the frontier moved. Vol. 01 wore the newsroom, Vol. 02 wore the clinic, and Vol. 03 wore the uniform, then four more costumes at once. Vol. 04 wears nothing: it sets each work beside its own earlier edition and lets you look.Every time a new class of Claude comes out, I have it redesign this entire site. The old version gets retired and archived, not deleted. Compare any two editions side by side and you can see how much better the models are getting: taste is harder to fake than a test score. This edition does the comparing for you.

What changed since Vol. 03 was issued in September, beyond the design. The five views are retired together and the site is one edition again, built from one record, so a count can no longer disagree with itself between pages. Great Books, AdmissionsAcademy, the music, Monitoring the Situation and Force Multipliers joined the work, and the Lab opened in October. Sixteen apps now have an earlier edition kept at its own address, most of them redesigned from a blank page by Claude Opus 5.5 in September. Every description was checked again on 9 November 2026 against the source and the live app, and corrected where the app had moved on or the claim was more than the record supports. The lines were recounted from source: 176,913 in 487 files, up from 150,906 in 247. Two things this edition adds that no earlier volume had: the work itself, shown as it looks today, and each earlier edition set beside it.Since the last edition: the five switchable designs were retired and the site is one design again. A medicine course was added, along with a tracker of what the internet is talking about, a set of how-to cards and a music page. Most of the apps were redesigned, and their old versions are still online. Every description was checked again against the real app. This edition also shows you a picture of every app, and can put the old version next to the new one.

How it was made, and how it is checked

This site documents seven months of learning and building in spare hours. These projects were made possible by tools that lowered the technical barrier between an idea and working software. Others will bring different experience and build better things.This site documents seven months of learning and building twenty-three working projects in spare time. New tools made it easier to turn ideas into software. Other people will bring different experience and build better things.

Everything listed is deployed and public. The site does not take that on faith: when a page loads, your browser asks every address to answer, and records what came back beside its name. The front page sets out the work by world, then the writing and the films. This page is the change record for the site itself.Everything here is online and public. When a page loads, it checks each app from your own browser and shows the result beside its name. The front page has the apps, then the writing and the videos. This page explains why the site keeps getting rebuilt.

Every system was specified by the author and written with Claude, then verified the way a clinician verifies a lab: against a source, by a second reader, and against the live deployment rather than the local copy. Medical content carries its citations. Scoring tables cite the governing publication. Where a source deck was wrong, the question was corrected and the correction logged.I describe what I want, the AI writes it, and then it gets checked the way a doctor checks a test result: against a trusted source, by a second reviewer, and on the real live site. Medical facts show where they came from.
