# Redraw brief: acid-base (Lesson 11, "Acid, base and balance")

Audit date 2026-11-12. Shots: `scratchpad/audit/shots/acid-base/s01..s16` at t=0.35 and t=1.
Files: `lab/acid-base/app.js` (617 lines, all pictures plus the Clinical values and Explore tabs), `index.html`, `style.css` (6 lines).

## Overall verdict: 4/10

The lesson is about chemistry and regulation, so most of its pictures are diagrams, not anatomy. That is fair. But the diagrams look like early wireframes: flat filled circles for molecules, rounded pink rectangles for "blood", a 30-unit clip-art lung and kidney, a stick-figure seesaw whose pans float off the beam, and no shading or depth anywhere. The two places where anatomy should carry the idea (step 8, kidney tubule cells, and step 5, where carbon dioxide leaves the blood for the lungs) show only an organ icon or a box. Pictures 6, 7, 8 and 11 to 16 (ten of the sixteen steps) are one seesaw template, and each frame leaves a band about 150 units tall, between the notes and the beam, empty.

The seesaw metaphor is good teaching, and it is also what Osmosis and Ninja Nerd use for Henderson-Hasselbalch. Keep it, but rebuild it as a real object, and put the organ that acts (lung, kidney tubule cell, brainstem) on the picture as a proper drawing instead of an icon.

## Global problems (fix once, in shared helpers)

1. **Seesaw construction.** In `seesaw()` and `mini()` the pans are horizontal strokes at `L.y-4` and `R.y-4`. The beam is a 7-wide stroke drawn on its own, so when the beam tilts its end pokes past or under the pan (see s07, s13, s14, s16). Pans need hangers or a cradle that rotates with the beam, and they should stay level, like a balance scale.
2. **The ghost beam collides with tokens.** `data-ghost="normal"` is a dashed line, `M66 300 H414`, and it runs through the bottom row of tokens whenever a pan drops (s07 right, s11 left, s14 right, s15 right). That breaks the rule that labels and lines never overlap things they do not belong to. Draw the ghost as a full dashed outline of the level beam and pans, behind everything, and keep the token stacks clear of it. Alternatively, raise the stacks so the lowest token sits above `PIV.y - 14`.
3. **Ringed "added" tokens overlap their neighbours.** `token(... added)` draws `r + 4` rings at a 26 gap, so neighbouring rings touch and cross (s08, s10, s14, s15). Either draw "added" as a token with a thin white inner ring plus a small "+" (no outer ring), or widen the gap for the added row to at least `2 * (TOK + 4) + 2`.
4. **The named change is missing on step 14 at t=1.** `meter()` only draws the arrow and word when `|n - g0| > 22`. For the diuretic, compensation moves the needle less than 22 units, so the finished picture has no words, which breaks FORMAT's "Changes" rule. For every changeScene, always name stage 2, even for a short move: draw the word without an arrow, or with a short fixed arrow.
5. **A stage-2 arrow starts from nothing.** In stage 2, `from = model(a)`, so the arrow starts at the stage-1 needle position, but nothing is drawn there (s11, s13, s15, s16). Draw a second, lighter ghost needle at the stage-1 position (a solid outline in muted grey, labelled "1"), so the arrow runs from one marker to the other. On s16 the ghost needle and the real needle also overlap at t=1. Offset the ghost below the bar, or draw the normal ghost as a tick mark above the bar.
6. **Dead space.** Every seesaw frame is 398 tall, with notes at y 110 to 160 and tokens starting near y 250. Pull the meter, notes and seesaw into a tighter vertical rhythm (meter 0 to 95, notes 100 to 190, seesaw 200 to 340, legend 350 to 395), and use the space for a larger organ drawing.
7. **The labels "blood" and "in blood" have a light halo** (`on-white` / paint-order stroke) on the pink fill (s02, s04, s05). It reads as a rendering glitch. Remove the stroke, or place the label outside the vessel.
8. **Icons are clip art.** `lungsIcon` and `kidneyIcon` are about 60 units across with a 2-unit flat outline. `acidIcon` and `plusIcon` are circles with dots. Replace them with the shaded primitives below.
9. **Molecule tokens are bare discs.** H+ is a 5-unit red dot, bicarbonate is a blue disc, carbonic acid is a pale disc with a red dot, and CO2 is a white ring. Keep the colour code, which is consistent and good, but give each a small radial gradient highlight. Optionally add a tiny glyph under 17 units as non-text decoration only (for example a "+" on H+, or "−" on bicarbonate drawn as a path, not as text). This makes the tokens read as ions rather than as dots.
10. **Ratio honesty.** The seesaw shows 6 vs 6 tokens as normal, while the step-6 caption says "about twenty bicarbonate to each dissolved carbon dioxide", and step 4 correctly draws 20 to 1. A student will see 6 = 6 and conclude the amounts are equal. Under each pan add a small fixed weight plate: "×20" under bicarbonate and "×1" under carbon dioxide (or say "each token is one sixth of its normal amount" next to the token legend). The model and the help text already say a token is one sixth of normal. Do NOT change the token count, because `stack()`, `tokenWords()` and the explore tab depend on six a side.

## Risks for whoever redraws
- **Model-driven geometry.** Tilt comes from `tiltOf(o.dpH)`, the needle from `needleFor(o)`, which uses the printed pH from `absolute()` and `K`, and the band from `bandX()`, which uses `K.phLo`/`K.phHi` and the same mapping. Tokens come from `stack(rel)` (six a side at normal, fractional opacity, lost rings, added beyond six). Redraw the look only. Do not change `model()`, `EFF`, `CHANGES`, `tiltOf`, `needleX` or the six-token convention, because the readouts, aria labels (`tokenWords`, `tiltWords`, `CHANGE_WHERE`) and the Clinical values status all agree with them.
- **Hooks used in this file.** `data-new-state` with `data-bicarb`, `data-co2` and `data-ph`; `data-needle`; `data-ghost-needle`; `data-ghost="normal"` (class `ghost`); `data-named`; `data-badge`; `data-note`; `data-lost`; `data-added`; `data-band`; `data-blood-band`; `data-co2`, `data-h` and `data-bicarb` on circles; and, in the explore map, `data-ph-band`, `data-point` and `data-primary`. No tool under `tools/` greps them today, but keep them for future checks and consistency with other lessons.
- **data-part names the captions use.** scale, co2, acids, h, buffer, bicarb, carbonic, enzyme, acid-side, base-side, answer, kidney (twice: cai and diuretic), added, drive. The parts lungs, kidneys and meter exist in the pictures but no dfn targets them. The `lab-check` minimum of 3 dfn per lesson is met.
- **Shared seesaw.** `seesaw()` serves steps 6, 7, 8, 11 to 16 and the Clinical values and Explore pictures. A layout change (heights, note positions) must be checked in all of them, at both 1280 and 375 x 553.
- **Ids.** The `svg()` helper rewrites `ah-` ids per picture (`p<n>-ah-`). Every new gradient, marker or clipPath id must start with `ah-`, or hidden steps lose their defs.
- **Type and labels.** Labels are at least 17 units. Watch two-badge frames (stage 2), where the left note "2 lungs answer:" and the right note "enzyme blocked: 1" meet near x 240. Adding bigger organ art there will collide. Budget the notes column at no more than 200 units a side.
- **Accuracy traps.** Carbonic anhydrase goes in red cells and tubule cells, not free plasma. Contraction alkalosis is the same bicarbonate in less fluid (do not draw bicarbonate being added by the kidney in stage 1). Salicylate badge 2 is a second primary disorder, not compensation. The lungs answer an alkalosis less well (EFF 0.5): do not exaggerate the stage-2 movement on steps 14 and 15. Do not name transporters, or print pH or PCO2 numbers, that the caption or clinical-values.json do not supply.
- **Ghost rule.** Each What-changes step must keep a dashed grey normal state and must name both stages in words with an arrow, including when the movement is small (step 14).
