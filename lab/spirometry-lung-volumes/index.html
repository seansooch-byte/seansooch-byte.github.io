What the model assumes

A single compartment empties exponentially. Normal emptying is tuned to an illustrative ratio of about 0.80. Compliance scales vital capacity; resistance slows emptying and can trap air. The opening flow rise is smoothed.

It does not model all airway mechanics, real patient variation, or diagnostic z-scores. Model volumes are illustrative; the clinical references are separate.

VC = 4800 × clamp(c, 0.4, 1.3)0.6. RV = 1200 × [1 + 0.6 × max(0,c−1)] + 400 × max(0,r−1.5)/2.5. τ = [−1/ln(0.2)] × r / max(1/c,0.3). FVC includes a resistance-dependent collapse term. V(t) = FVC × (1−e−t/τ).

TAKE IT WITH YOU

A breath, in perspective.

Educational, not for clinical use.

  • A spirometer sees moving air. Residual air stays inside, so residual volume and capacities containing it need another method.
  • Read the total forced breath and the part exhaled early together. Obstruction slows emptying; restriction reduces total lung capacity.
  • More compliance means easier stretch, not necessarily better emptying. Emphysema loses recoil; fibrosis makes inflation difficult.
  • A bronchodilator response measures a change. It can occur in asthma and COPD and is not a stand-alone diagnosis.
