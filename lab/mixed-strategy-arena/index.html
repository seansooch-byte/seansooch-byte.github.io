Two simple learning rules play the game against themselves. Neither knows the answer, yet their average play drifts to the equilibrium. Exploitability measures how much a perfect opponent could win from the current average strategies, and it falls toward 0.

Fictitious play

Each side best-responds to the other's whole history so far.

Regret matching
